## 1. Story Concept & Setting

In humid, neon-soaked **1999 Kanda-Akihabara**, a burned-out basement electronics engineer builds a jury-rigged analog **"Phase-Tape" transceiver** out of broadcast vacuum tubes to overwrite a single 13-second window from the night his sister vanished. Instead of producing a clean rewrite, the loop tears open **Channel 13**—a dead frequency where erased timelines condense into raw kinetic willpower (**Static Resonance**).

When a shadowy continuity syndicate—**The Archivist Bureau**—dispatches elite "Sweepers" to erase the shop and everyone inside, the engineer's only shield is a street brawler who survived deletion and came back punching through static.

**Showcase Scene ("Rain on the Glass" — Shibuya Broadcast Tower, 2:13 AM)**:  
A rain-lashed glass atrium and narrow steel catwalk beneath a shattered stained-glass skylight, lined with dead CRT monitors, hanging coaxial cables, and oil-slick puddles reflecting magenta-and-cobalt neon.

## 2. Visual Style Lock (Hand-Drawn Cel Anti-Slop Standard)

- **Studio Craft Fusion**: Authentic 1990s hand-inked cel animation blending:
  - *Cowboy Bebop* wet-steel specular reflections, loose martial-arts weight, and stained-glass noir ambiance
  - *Bungo Stray Dogs* steep Dutch angles and bold angular shadows
  - *Yu Yu Hakusho* high-contrast black-and-white sakuga impact frames
  - *Steins;Gate* warm amber Nixie-tube illumination vs. cold cyan CRT phosphor scanlines
- **Material & Film Texture**: Expressive heavy ink contours, gouache storm sky lighting, authentic 35mm anime film grain.
- **Strict Exclusions**: Zero 3D CGI, zero glossy plastic skin shading, zero sterile modern airbrush gradients.

## 3. Original Recast Character Locks

Use these exact visual descriptors in all prompts for consistent multi-cut framing:

- **Renji Takanashi (19)**: Wiry street brawler, swept-back messy jet-black hair with one stray forehead strand, open **faded forest-olive work jacket** over a white tank top, white-taped knuckles, crooked smirk; loose Jeet Kune Do / boxing footwork paired with cobalt-white **"Pulse-Shot"** knuckle shockwaves.
- **Toma Kurose (22)**: Tall gaunt engineer, disheveled dark-brown hair, **grease-stained white mechanic's shop coat** over a charcoal turtleneck, cracked round wire-frame glasses, brass-and-glass **amber Nixie-tube forearm Phase Counter (`13.000s`)**.
- **Sora Hayakawa (21)**: Sharp amber-hazel eyes, straight **chestnut-copper hair** clipped at one side, **camel-tan trench blazer** over an ivory blouse and loose **burgundy silk tie**, carrying a portable spectrum analyzer.
- **Kuroha ("Vesper")**: Compact predatory build, raven-black hair with a **silver-white temple shock**, torn **charcoal collared mantle**, glowing **crimson scarred eye** beneath a dark brow wrap, linen-wrapped carbon-black wakizashi trailing roaring **obsidian-violet "Dead-Fire"**.
- **Masato Kurosawa ("The Metronome")**: Tailored **charcoal three-piece pinstripe suit**, wine-red silk pocket square, slicked **silver-grey hair**, thin obsidian sunglasses, black leather gloves; wields **"Zero Beat"** (freezes falling raindrops/inertia in a 15m radius and frame-skips into blind spots).
