## ACT II — The 13-Second Rule (`0:52 – 1:47`, `~55s`, 5 New Clips)

*Fade down from Act I `DEAD CHANNEL 13` title smash into low rain, warm amber vacuum tubes, and electrical crackle.*

### Scene II-1 (`ep1_06_basement_triage`) — INT. TOMA'S AKIHABARA BASEMENT SHOP — NIGHT
Smoke curls lazily from Toma's brass Nixie wrist gauntlet as amber tubes flicker violently between `13.000` and `13.001`. Renji leans against a cracked workbench, wrapping fresh white tape over static-scalded knuckles. Behind him, a dead CRT monitor briefly reflects blue ghost scanlines across half his face.
- **Renji (JP — <=18 mora):** 「おいチューナー……俺の拳、さっき透けて見えたか？」
- **Renji (EN):** *"Hey, Tuner... did my fist just turn translucent a second ago?"*

### Scene II-2 (`ep1_07_sora_warning`) — INT. SHOP WORKBENCH — CONTINUOUS
Sora slams her portable spectrum analyzer beside a glowing green wireframe map of Tokyo. Dozens of crimson triangulating pings converge directly on Kanda-Akihabara. Below the map ticks an auxiliary Bureau header: **`Y2K ANALOG PURGE: 06D 21H`**.
- **Sora (JP — <=20 mora):** 「13秒を超えたせいで、東京中に信号が漏れてる！」
- **Sora (EN):** *"Crossing thirteen seconds flooded every frequency across Tokyo with our signal!"*

### Scene II-3 (`ep1_08_yuki_tape_flashback`) — INSERT / CRT PLAYBACK — MEMORY `02:13 AM`
Toma hits PLAY on his dusty silver cassette deck. Every CRT on the shelf snaps into sharp cyan scanlines: **Yuki Kurose (17)** stands frozen under rain at a deserted `02:13 AM` Shibuya crosswalk, red hairclip glinting beneath a fractured neon sign, hand pressed to the glass of a telephone booth as static eats at her silhouette.
- **Yuki (JP — <=20 mora, Radio EQ):** 「兄さん……チャンネル13を、消させないで！」
- **Yuki (EN — Radio EQ):** *"Toma... don't let them erase Channel 13!"*

### Scene II-4 (`ep1_09_shutter_melt`) — INT. SHOP FRONT — PURE SILENCE & ACTION
Audio drops cleanly into **total vacuum silence** (`0.25s`). The corrugated steel security shutter at the basement stairs suddenly bisects along a razor-thin, white-hot **obsidian-violet diagonal line**. Zero sound until the top half shears and crashes onto wet concrete in a shower of indigo sparks.
- **Action-Only Clip:** Pure SFX + ambient cut (no spoken dialogue).

### Scene II-5 (`ep1_10_kuroha_steps_in`) — INT. BASEMENT DOORWAY — NIGHT
Rain whips inward past the fallen steel shutter. **Kuroha** steps lazily out of the violet steam, trailing blackened purple embers off his lowered wakizashi, scarred crimson eye locking straight onto Toma's smoking Nixie gauntlet.
- **Kuroha (JP — <=18 mora):** 「その時計をよこせ、チューナー。俺が借りるぞ。」
- **Kuroha (EN):** *"Hand over that watch, Tuner. I'm taking it from here."*

## ACT III — Dead-Fire in the Alley (`1:47 – 2:47`, `~60s`, 6 New Clips)

*148 BPM aggressive synth-bass & drum break surges back in.*

### Scene III-1 (`ep1_11_alley_breakout`) — EXT. AKIHABARA CRT BACK-ALLEY — NIGHT
Renji lunges straight across the threshold before Kuroha can swing, shoulder-checking the swordsman backwards through hanging plastic tarps and out into a cramped, neon-drenched back-alley stacked high with dumped CRT televisions and rain puddles.
- **Action-Only Clip:** Fast tracking whip-pan breakout (enhanced with `1.6x` on-twos burst & water-splash SFX).

### Scene III-2 (`ep1_12_sakuga_slash_flurry`) — EXT. RAIN ALLEY — SAKUGA SEQUENCE
*(High-speed sakuga pass: `1.70x–2.30x` on-twos speed ramps + `3f` Hakukoma/Kurokoma Hit-Stop).* Kuroha unleashes three blind-speed **obsidian-violet *Dead-Fire*** crescent waves that slice sopping umbrellas, metal vending machines, and stacked CRT husks cleanly in half. Renji slips millimeters beneath each blade arc in tight low southpaw bobs, water vapor exploding behind him.
- **Action-Only Clip:** Zero dialogue; pure animation & metallic blade-whoosh / vapor SFX.

### Scene III-3 (`ep1_13_deadfire_nullify`) — EXT. RAIN ALLEY — CLASH
Renji snaps back inside Kuroha's guard and drives a roaring cobalt-white **Pulse-Shot** straight toward his ribs. Instead of blocking, Kuroha reverses his wakizashi into the blue lightning itself—*Dead-Fire* **devours and cauterizes the cobalt static into violet ash**, blasting Renji backward into a mountain of shattered CRT glass.
- **Action-Only Clip:** High-contrast crimson/violet flash cut + glass-crush sub impact.

### Scene III-4 (`ep1_14_kuroha_taunt`) — EXT. RAIN ALLEY — CLOSE-UP
Sora cries out off-frame as Renji tries to force himself upright through smoking tape burns. Kuroha stands over him, violet flames wreathing the wakizashi tip near Renji's throat, scarred red eye cold and steady.
- **Kuroha (JP — <=20 mora):** 「消された幽霊が、黒炎に勝てると思うな、七番。」
- **Kuroha (EN):** *"A deleted ghost can't beat Dead-Fire. Your time ran out, Number Seven."*

### Scene III-5 (`ep1_15_toma_overclock_step`) — EXT. ALLEY ENTRANCE — RAIN
Toma sprints out through the sheared shutter doorway into the rain, tearing the brass safety latch off his forearm Phase-Tape while Sora grabs vainly at his lab coat shoulder in sheer terror.
- **Sora (JP — <=20 mora):** 「止めて拓真！13.001を超えたら、あんたまで消える！」
- **Sora (EN):** *"Stop, Toma! Cross 13.001 again and you'll erase yourself!"*

### Scene III-6 (`ep1_16_nixie_overclock_dial`) — MACRO ECU — PHASE-TAPE OVERCLOCK
Extreme macro close-up: Toma jams the brass toggle past the cracked red limit pin. Amber Nixie digits blur wildly (`13.000` → `13.001` → **`13.130`**) as white-hot cyan and gold electricity arcs across Toma's fingers—and the falling raindrops suddenly begin **rising upward**.
- **Action-Only Clip:** Accelerating high-pitch tube whine -> `0.22s` **Total Vacuum Cutout**.

## ACT IV — Channel 13 Opens (`2:47 – 3:47`, `~60s`, 4 New Clips + End Card)

*Vacuum snaps open into massive `5f` Hakukoma/Kurokoma ink impact + full distorted synth-brass climax.*

### Scene IV-1 (`ep1_17_time_tear_counter`) — EXT. RAIN ALLEY — CLIMAX SAKUGA
Kuroha whips around at a static sonic boom—to face **two overlapping silhouettes of Renji**: the battered present Renji low on one knee, and a **3-second erased alternate future pulled straight out of Channel 13** corkscrewing overhead from dead air! Both cobalt **Pulse-Shot** fists detonate against Kuroha's crossed blade at the exact same millisecond, blowing a blinding cyan shockwave down the entire alley.
- **Action-Only Clip:** Peak sakuga combo (`1.8x–2.3x` on-twos + `5f` Hit-Stop + `dandadan_cyan`/`dandadan_crimson` strobe flashes).

### Scene IV-2 (`ep1_18_kuroha_retreat`) — EXT. HIGH ALLEY NEON SIGNBOARD — NIGHT
Ten meters up on a rain-slick kanji neon sign, Kuroha lands crouched, wiping a thin line of blood from his cheek as half the alley streetlights explode below. Instead of rage, his scarred eye glints with razor-sharp fascination before violet static swallows him whole.
- **Kuroha (JP — <=18 mora):** 「なるほど……消された未来すら引きずり出すか。」
- **Kuroha (EN):** *"I see... so you can drag erased futures straight out of dead air."*

### Scene IV-3 (`ep1_19_yuki_signal_twist`) — EXT. RAIN ALLEY — THE BROADCAST TWIST
Toma slumps to his knees in the puddle, gasping as `13.130` cools back to `13.000`. All around him and Renji, twenty shattered alley CRTs suddenly blink alive **in crystal-clear high definition**—no static snow, no distortion. Standing inside a towering white marble vault lined with cold digital servers, **Yuki Kurose** presses both palms against the inner glass, staring straight into Toma's eyes while silver-suited guards move behind her.
- **Yuki (JP — <=20 mora):** 「兄さん……私、生きてる。渋谷のコアの中にいるの。」
- **Yuki (EN):** *"Toma... I'm alive. I'm trapped inside the Shibuya Core."*

### Scene IV-4 (`ep1_20_masato_broadcast_hook`) — INT. SHIBUYA BROADCAST TOWER SUMMIT — NIGHT
Wide-to-tight dolly inside the towering dark command deck high above rain-soaked Tokyo. Beneath a colossal amber LED wall reading **`Y2K DIGITAL CUTOVER // 06 DAYS 21:47:00`**, **Masato Kurosawa** watches Toma & Yuki's live screen lock flicker across the central monitor, pulling a crisp fresh black leather glove tightly over his right hand.
- **Masato (JP — <=18 mora):** 「パージ発令。神田秋葉原を、テープごと消去せよ。」
- **Masato (EN):** *"Initiate Purge Protocol. Wipe Kanda-Akihabara off the tape."*

### End Card (`ep1_end_card`, `4.0s`)
Slam back to lonely pattering rain + muted Dm9 Rhodes decay:
