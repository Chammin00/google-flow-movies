## 2. Canonical Character Continuity Locks

### Hero 1 — Toad as Ryu Hayabusa ("Dragon Toad")
- **Silhouette & Head:** Heroic compact 3.5-foot mushroom-headed warrior, unmistakable **white bulbous mushroom cap with bold red polka-dot spots**, fierce determined big dark eyes, theatrical 3D animated character proportions.
- **Shinobi Armor & Gear:** Ryu Hayabusa's sleek matte-black and dark-indigo armored dragon-clan shinobi suit — quilted navy tactical bodysuit, sculpted gunmetal bracers wrapped with crimson cord, **dark metallic falcon headband tied around the base of his red-spotted white mushroom cap** with **two long tattered blue-and-crimson scarf tails** whipping in the wind, armored shinobi tabi boots.
- **Signature Weapon:** The ornate **Dragon Sword katana** (gold dragon-eye hilt, glowing azure-gold tempered Damascus steel blade) carried in a back scabbard.

### Hero 2 — Toadette as Ayane ("Blossom Toadette")
- **Silhouette & Head:** Agile compact pink mushroom-headed kunoichi, unmistakable **rosy-pink bulbous mushroom cap with bright white polka-dot spots**, iconic **twin braided pink mushroom pigtails** extending from the back of her cap tied with flowing **lavender-purple silk shinobi ribbons**, fierce bright eyes, confident smirk, theatrical 3D animated character proportions.
- **Kunoichi Attire & Gear:** Ayane's royal-purple and plum shinobi kunoichi attire — deep-violet silk sleeveless ninja kimono-tunic with gold embroidery and crimson cord lacing, **large sculpted amethyst butterfly-wing obi sash** at her back with long trailing purple silk ribbons, dark shinobi arm-guards and leg wraps.
- **Signature Weapons:** Twin curved silver **Fuma Kodachi** short-blades with purple-wrapped hilts and glowing **magenta-violet cherry-blossom explosive kunai**.

### Antagonists
- **Shadow-Shell Foot Ninjas:** Armored turtle-shell shadow ninjas with spiked matte-obsidian shells, glowing crimson eye slits under dark steel *kasa* hats, swinging chains and black katana.
- **Lord Kuro-Kame (Obsidian Dragon-Shell Shogun):** Towering 12-foot demonic armored turtle-oni warlord in molten-cracked black samurai armor, colossal volcano-spiked obsidian back shell glowing red-hot, horned iron kabuto helmet, wielding a massive rune-etched greatsword crackling with crimson lightning.
